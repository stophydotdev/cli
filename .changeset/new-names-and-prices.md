Follow the new API. To point at one thing, give its link or its id as the argument, such as `stophy youtube video M4TufsFlv_o`, or use the matching option like `--videoUrl` or `--videoId`. Send one, not both. Transcripts are now `stophy youtube transcript`, `stophy instagram transcript` and `stophy tiktok transcript`, and search, news, maps, Play, flights and trends live under `stophy google`. Amazon commands are new. Page-numbered commands no longer show `hasMore`: run the next page until a page has no results.
